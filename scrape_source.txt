---

# 1. Google News RSS (MOST IMPORTANT)

## Why

This is the BEST hackathon source.

You can monitor:

```text
Funding
Leadership changes
Acquisitions
Expansion
AI adoption
Cloud migration
Product launches
Partnerships
Layoffs
```

---

## Example

```text
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Microsoft+AI+cloud+leadership
```

OR

```text
https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Salesforce+funding+cloud+acquisition
```

---

## Why powerful

Google aggregates:

* news websites
* press releases
* blogs
* media publications

So:

```text
1 RSS feed → many sources
```

---

## What to scrape

```text
Article title
Article link
Published date
Snippet
```

---

## Triggers detectable

```text

Cloud migration
Hiring
```

4. Store content

```text
faster
safer
more scalable
```
