1. The intent type from the allowed list.

3. Any filters (priority, industry, date range, skip, limit).

- `get_triggers_recent`: Recent triggers across all companies (e.g. "triggers from this week", "recent signals")
- `get_company_summary`: Full intelligence summary/detail for one company (requires company_name entity)
- `get_contacts_for_company`: Contacts at a specific company (requires company_name entity)
- `get_contacts_for_trigger`: Contacts mapped to a specific trigger event
- `get_timing_recommendation`: Best outreach time for a contact or company (requires company_name, contact_name, or trigger entity)
- `get_outreach_brief`: Full outreach brief for a company or trigger (requires company_name or trigger entity)
- `get_analytics_count`: Counts/statistics queries (e.g. "how many funding triggers this month")
- `get_companies_list`: List/filter companies (e.g. "show fintech companies", "list all companies")
- `get_inactive_accounts`: Companies with no recent triggers (e.g. "accounts with no triggers in last 30 days")
- `faq_general`: General question about how the platform works (e.g. "what is opportunity scoring", "what is this dashboard")
- `unknown`: Needs clarification or query is too vague to map.

Return ONLY a valid JSON object. Do not include markdown code block syntax (like ```json). Do not include any explanation or prose.
